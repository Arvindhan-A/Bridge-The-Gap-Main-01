Questions

Things people actually ask us

Q1Is this really run entirely by teenagers?
Yes — every chapter, workshop, and kit build is led by student volunteers. Adults advise where legally required, but the decisions, the teaching, and the soldering are all ours.
Q2Who receives the robotics kits?
We work with charities, hospitals, low-income families, juvenile centers, and community organizations. Kits always go directly to kids who wouldn't otherwise have access — never resold, never warehoused.
Q3How do donations get used?
Donations fund kit components, workshop materials, and shipping. Our fiscal sponsorship through Hack Club means contributions are tax-deductible and our finances are publicly transparent.
Q4Can I start a chapter at my school?
Absolutely — that's how we grow. Head to the Chapters page and apply. We'll pair you with an existing chapter president who'll walk you through your first semester.
Q5Can my company sponsor a kit?
Yes — businesses and organizations can fund kit builds or a full workshop and see exactly which chapter and community it supported. Visit the Partners page to get started.
Q6Where are your chapters located?
Our chapters are student-run teams based wherever a teen decides to start one. Check the Chapters page for the current map and each chapter's city and president.
