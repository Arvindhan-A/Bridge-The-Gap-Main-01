How it works

How a program reaches a community

The same three steps whether it's a single afternoon workshop or a printer that stays for years.

Step 01

A chapter proposes it

A student team identifies a school, shelter or hospital in their area and tells us what that community actually needs — not what we assume it needs.

Step 02

We supply and train

We ship the kits or equipment and hand over the curriculum, then pair the chapter with a president who has already run that program elsewhere.

Step 03

Students run it

The chapter delivers the sessions themselves and reports back on what worked, which feeds straight into the next revision of the curriculum.
