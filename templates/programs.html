What We Run

Programs

Everything Bridge the Gap runs to bring hands-on STEM to communities — from preschool demos to student-led research.

Early Learners

Preschool Demos

We partner with preschools and afterschool programs to bring hands-on STEM to the youngest learners — through simple, playful builds like paper bridges and paper-craft stick structures that turn abstract ideas into something kids can hold.

Maker Infrastructure

LaunchPad

Our 3D printer–focused initiative, pairing printers with starter kits for chapters, libraries, and Title I schools to build a sustainable, hands-on maker program that keeps producing long after we leave.

Entrepreneurship

Young Founders Lab

An entrepreneurship lab for youth, taught by high school mentors, teaching students how to develop startups and pitch their ideas — the business side of building something real.

Homeschool Community

Homeschool STEAM Webinars

Monthly webinars, each spotlighting a letter of STEAM, connecting core concepts to real robotics applications — developed in partnership with homeschool communities.

Curriculum Partnership

Government School Curriculum

Open-source robotics curriculum built for government and public school classrooms, designed to run on affordable materials and to be taught by teachers with no prior robotics background.
